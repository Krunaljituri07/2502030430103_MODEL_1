Our Royal Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence Since 2009

15+ Years of Excellence

From a Royal Vision
to a Global Empire

Royal Feast was born from a singular dream — to recreate the magnificence of Mughal palace kitchens in the modern world. Founded in 2009 by Master Chef Rajan Khanna, we set out to prove that food is not merely sustenance, but ceremony.

From our first intimate 12-seat dining room in Mumbai, we have grown to serve over 12,000 royal guests annually across 5 cities. Every recipe is a legacy — some dating back 400 years — preserved and elevated for the modern connoisseur.
